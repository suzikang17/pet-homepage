# Overview

## Brand

**Homepage** — at **`homepage.pet`**.

The name channels the MySpace / Xanga / GeoCities era of personal websites. Every pet gets their own homepage at a subdomain they own: `sandy.homepage.pet`, `max.homepage.pet`. The URL itself is the pitch — "Sandy's homepage, pet."

## The big idea

**A homepage for your pet.** Pet owners forward vet bills, vaccination records, photos, and notes to their pet's dedicated email and text number. AI organizes everything into a beautiful living webpage, sends proactive reminders, and generates vet visit prep summaries on demand. Hardware-agnostic — no collar required.

## One-liner

> A homepage for your pet. Forward the stuff. We'll remember it for them.

## Why this, why now

- Pet owners increasingly treat pets like family members — willing to pay for tools that serve the pet
- Existing pet records solutions are either ugly utilitarian apps (PetDesk, VitusVet, VetDex) or hardware-gated ecosystems (Fi, Tractive)
- AI document parsing makes email ingestion genuinely viable
- Nobody has built a beautiful, software-only, web-first pet page with AI-generated vet prep and emotional memory features
- The emotional hook (memories, recap, year-in-review) is underserved in the category — everything currently feels clinical

## Target user

Dog and cat owners who already treat their pet like family. Specifically the segment that:

- Buys fancy food, toys, accessories without thinking
- Has group chats and camera rolls full of pet photos
- Experiences mild anxiety about forgetting vet appointments
- Would pay for (or already pays for) pet insurance

Demographics skew 28–45, urban/suburban, above-average income, tech-comfortable but not developer-tier. Slight skew toward women for the emotional-connection-to-pet-products segment.

## Key design principles

1. **Beautiful > feature-complete.** The Greece-trip-quality bar. Every screen should feel loved, not engineered.
2. **Zero-friction input.** People should barely feel they're "using" the product. Forward an email. Send a text. Done.
3. **Web-first and shareable.** Not an app silo. A URL you can send to the sitter, the vet, or grandma.
4. **Hardware-agnostic.** No collar required. Works for any pet, any owner.
5. **Emotional + practical.** Recap and memories sit alongside vet prep and reminders. Both matter.

## What this is NOT

- A wearable / hardware product (at least not at first — see [[10-hardware-vision]])
- A clinical vet records system for practices (see [[11-vet-partnerships]])
- A social network for pet owners
- A marketplace for pet products
- A generic "pet app" that does everything shallowly

## Wedge and moat (short version)

- **Wedge:** email ingestion as the primary input mechanism — no competitor has made this the core onboarding flow. 6-18 month head start.
- **Moat:** distribution, not product. Shareable `<petname>.homepage.pet` URLs, SEO compounding, and accumulated memory data create the long-term defensibility.
- **Delayed moat:** after 2-3 years of use, a pet's Homepage contains irreplaceable memory data. Leaving costs emotional history, not just a subscription.
