# Problem and Users

## The problem

Pet owners accumulate tons of information about their pets over the pet's lifetime — vet bills, vaccination certificates, medication schedules, photos, funny moments, behavioral notes, insurance documents, grooming receipts. Today this information is scattered across:

- Gmail inbox (vet bills, insurance, appointment reminders)
- Camera roll (photos and videos)
- Vet clinic portals (each vet uses a different system, often inaccessible)
- Paper folders (printed documents, receipts)
- Group chats / iMessage threads (casual observations, photos shared with family)
- Scraps of paper (feeding schedules taped to the fridge)
- Memory (the half-remembered things you'd tell the vet if you could)

## Concrete pain points

1. **Vet visits are stressful and under-prepared.** You know Max has been acting weird, but you forgot exactly when it started, how many times it happened, and what the vet said last time. You walk out of appointments realizing you forgot to ask three things.
2. **Vaccine and preventative lapses are common.** Monthly preventatives (heartworm, flea/tick) get missed. Annual vaccinations lapse because nobody remembers when they were last given.
3. **Sitters and helpers get inadequate info.** When you travel, you send a hasty text to the sitter with care instructions you forget to update. Emergency info is incomplete.
4. **Pet memories get lost.** Years of photos sit on phones with no narrative. Nobody looks back at them. Funny moments get forgotten.
5. **Multi-vet households are chaos.** If you see a specialist, an ER vet, and a primary care vet, the three don't talk to each other. You're the integrator, badly.
6. **Records requests are painful.** Changing vets, pet insurance claims, boarding facilities — all require assembling records that are scattered across half a dozen places.

## Target user personas

### The primary user: "Sandi's person"

- 30-something pet parent, urban or suburban
- Treats pet like a family member
- Already spends generously on pet stuff (good food, toys, insurance)
- Phone is full of pet photos
- Experiences low-grade anxiety about forgetting appointments
- Comfortable with tech but not a power user of productivity tools
- Willing to pay for something that makes their pet's life better

### The secondary user: "The multi-pet caretaker"

- Has 2+ pets (often a mix: 2 dogs, a cat and a dog, etc.)
- Even higher pain around organization
- More likely to have experienced pet health issues
- Higher willingness to pay

### The occasional user: "The sitter / family member"

- Doesn't own the pet but temporarily responsible
- Needs access to instructions and records when watching the pet
- Should be able to view and add to the page without signing up
- Sees the product through someone else's account — a potential virality vector

## What users do today (the current workarounds)

- Notion pages they abandon after two weeks
- Google Docs shared with partners
- iCloud shared albums for photos
- Vet clinic portals (frustrating, siloed, forgotten)
- Paper folders in a filing cabinet
- Calendar reminders for preventatives (half the time ignored)
- Text threads with the sitter
- Trying to remember
