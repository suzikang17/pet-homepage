**Free-form moments — a note, some photos, no structure required — plus one grid of every photo ever attached to anything.**

## How it works
A diary entry is just a date, an optional note, and photos. It's the default outcome of camera capture ("Note only") and one tap from the Timeline "+" menu. Entries appear in the Timeline stream under the Diary chip. The **Photos** toggle on the Timeline swaps to a grid of every photo across all record types — diary, activities, vet visits, vaccines, meds.

## Details
- The former Diary tab was merged into the Timeline (chip + Photos view mode); nothing was lost in the merge.
- A diary entry can be "promoted" conceptually by capture tagging — the same photo+note tagged as an activity or dose becomes that record instead.
