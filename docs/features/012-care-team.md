**Your pet's people — vets and clinics with contact details — attachable to any medical record.**

## How it works
The Care Team tab lists veterinarians (name, clinic, phone, email, website, address, notes). "Import from Contacts" fills a vet from the system contact picker in one tap — no Contacts permission needed (the picker runs out-of-process and returns only the tapped contact). Vets are attachable to vaccinations, vet visits, and medications, and show on those records' detail pages.

## Details
- Contact import maps name/organization/phone/email/website/postal address automatically; everything stays editable.
