**When something's wrong, open an episode and track it until it resolves — with dated check-ins you can show the vet.**

## How it works
A symptom **episode** has a category (digestive, skin, mobility, …), an optional title, a start date, and a status (Active/Resolved). While it's open, dated **check-in entries** record severity (mild/moderate/severe), notes, and suspected causes. Resolving stamps the end date. The Timeline shows episodes under the Symptoms chip with their Active/Resolved state.

## Details
- Episodes are spans, not moments — which is why they're the one record type not offered from camera capture.
- Check-ins live on the episode's detail page.
