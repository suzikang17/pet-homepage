**Keep every shot on file — name, lot number, who gave it — and never miss the next one.**

## How it works
A vaccination records the vaccine name, date administered, optional lot number / administered-by / attending vet, and photos of the certificate. An optional next-due date puts it in Home's "Due soon" and schedules a one-shot reminder for that day ("Rabies is due for Milo"). Vaccines arrive three ways: typed in manually, extracted automatically by scanning a vet document (AI), or from a captured photo via the Vaccine handoff.

## Details
- Deleting a vaccination cancels its pending reminder.
- Rows render in the Timeline under the Vaccines chip with their next-due badge.
