**Numeric health tracking — weight, appetite, energy, water, temperature — with the series over time.**

## How it works
A marker is one measurement: a type (weight/appetite/energy/water/temperature/other), a value, an optional unit, and a date. Home shows the latest weight as a tile with quick-add; the time-ordered series per type drives the trend view. Markers can be logged inline from camera capture (pick the type, key the number).

## Details
- Marker rows show as "Weight: 12.4 kg"-style entries in the Timeline under the Health chip.
- No reminders — markers are measurements, not scheduled events.
